## Recommendation

Treat the cache as a stable, low-fan-out module with one hot consumer: keep changes internal to `eqproxy-net/src/cache.rs` where possible, and if the public names in `eqproxy-net/src/lib.rs:47-50` or the `Lookup`/`FetchOutcome`/`StoredParts`/`CacheSettings` shapes must change, update `serve_cached` (`eqproxy-net/src/http1.rs:1970`) and the assertions in `eqproxy-net/tests/e2e_cache.rs` in the same commit, since the four protocol front ends all funnel through that path.
