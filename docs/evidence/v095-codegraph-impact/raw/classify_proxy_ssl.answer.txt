## What actually breaks

**Callers that must keep compiling:** only the two above — a signature change touches `http_directive` and `server` and nothing else.

**Callees whose contract it fixes (Ce=8), `./eqproxy-config/src/import/nginx.rs`:**

- `directive` (`:286`), `proxy_ssl_toggle` (`:2992`), `proxy_ssl_path` (`:3003`, called twice), `proxy_ssl_scope_mapped` (`:3276`), `unknown_proxy_ssl` (`:3264`), `proxy_ssl_scope_lost` (`:3300`)
- `Conv::info` (`./eqproxy-config/src/import/mod.rs:454`) and `Conv::review` (`./eqproxy-config/src/import/mod.rs:465`)

**Shared-helper risk:** `proxy_ssl_toggle` and `proxy_ssl_path` are also used by `collect_proxy_ssl` (`:3012`), which is called from `http` (`:410`) and `server` (`:1261`). Any behavior change to those helpers alters both the *notes* produced by `classify_proxy_ssl` and the *inherited defaults* silently collected by `collect_proxy_ssl` — two different outputs from one edit.

**Note-text coupling (the fragile one):** `downgrade_unfulfilled_proxy_ssl_server_name` (`./eqproxy-config/src/import/nginx.rs:387-406`, called by `convert`) post-processes notes produced here by matching `note.severity == Severity::Done && note.directive.starts_with("proxy_ssl_server_name")`. If you change the severity, the directive text format, or which branch emits `Done`, that downgrade silently stops applying and SNI-requested-without-DNS output changes.

**Scope boundary to be aware of:** `location` (`./eqproxy-config/src/import/nginx.rs`) is *not* a caller — it has its own `proxy_ssl_*` handling via `proxy_ssl_toggle` and `unknown_proxy_ssl`. A fix or extension made in `classify_proxy_ssl` does not reach location scope.

**Tests that pin current behavior (not production):** `inherited_sni_without_a_dns_name_is_not_done` (`:4173`) asserts an `Info`/`sin nombre DNS` note when SNI is inherited without a DNS name; `unknown_proxy_ssl_is_explicitly_classified_in_every_scope` (`:4193`) asserts unknown `proxy_ssl_*` yields exactly one `Review` note per scope. Both drive the path through `convert` → `http` → `http_directive` → `classify_proxy_ssl`.

## Recommendation

A signature change is cheap (`I=0.8`, two callers, one file). The real hazard is not the signature but the *note contract*: keep the directive string prefix and the `Done` severity for `proxy_ssl_server_name`, or update `downgrade_unfulfilled_proxy_ssl_server_name` (`:387-406`) in the same change. Also remember `location` scope and `collect_proxy_ssl` share helpers with this function.
