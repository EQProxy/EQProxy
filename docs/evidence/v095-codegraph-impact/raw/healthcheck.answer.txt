## Branches inside the probe (all reached only from `healthcheck`)

- **`resolve_admin`** (`main.rs:2311-2358`) decides the branch: `Some(admin)` → **`probe_admin`** (`main.rs:1447-1470`), which connects to the admin TCP endpoint or the admin Unix socket and delegates to `exchange_admin`; `None` → **`probe_listeners`** (`main.rs:1506-1529`), which iterates `config.listener` and calls **`loopback_for`** (`main.rs:1531-1539`) before a `TcpStream::connect`. ## What actually breaks

- **`main`** (`main.rs:211-230`) — the only production caller. A signature change (extra param, `&Config` instead of `&Path`, different return type) breaks the `Command::Healthcheck` arm and the `ExitCode::from(code)` line. A change to the `0`/`1` exit convention breaks every process-level consumer (systemd/docker/K8s probes) and `main`'s exit mapping.
- Shared callees mean collateral damage, not callers: `load_and_check` is also called by `run`, `doctor`, `diff`, `plan`, `reload`, `explain` and `validate` (all in `main.rs`); `base_dir` is called by many of the same plus `plan`, `doctor`, `apply_candidate`, `gate`; `CheckResult::is_valid` has nine callers in `eqproxy-cli/src/main.rs`. `resolve_admin` is the one non-trivial exception: besides `healthcheck` it is also called by `gate`, so changes there hit a second code path.
- **Sole-caller helpers**, safe to change together with `healthcheck`: `probe_admin` (`main.rs:1447`) and `probe_listeners` (`main.rs:1506`) have `healthcheck` as their only caller. `loopback_for` is shared with `check_listener_warnings` and `check_admin`.
