# v0.9.5 / P0-3 + P0-4 — bodyless framing and `Connection` nomination

Date: 2026-11-11. Branch `release/v0.9.5`, base `1a7bf12`. The release tag
`v0.9.5` points at the commit that contains this file.

Artifact under test: `target/release/eqproxy`, `eqproxy 0.9.5`,
SHA-256 `29abadc1ac0f43975544b42a77f19fec458e644e41136492e1efa0955fd3904d`.

## Objective

Close P0-3 and P0-4 of the adversarial audit on the release tree:

- **ADV-01/P0-3:** a bodyless response (`1xx`/`204`/`304`/`HEAD`) that declares
  body framing must not leave a delayed body on a pooled backend connection.
- **ADV-02/P0-4:** headers nominated by `Connection` must not cross a
  translation. The h2/h3 translators filter the nominated names; the h1
  sanitizer does the same on the real HTTP/1.1 listener. A raw h2c message that
  carries `Connection` is rejected by the h2 protocol layer before translation.

## Changes

| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `eqproxy-net/src/http1.rs` | `ResponseHeadError::{Malformed, BodylessFraming}` and `parse_response_head_checked` keep the refusal reason. A bodyless TE, or CL on `1xx`/`204`, is `502`. `bodyless_backend_close` derives from the raw head that a `304`/`HEAD` with legal CL is not returned to the pool, while `wants_close` keeps the client connection usable. The HTTP/1.0 M-01 rewrite is preserved through `allow_bodyless_te`; the framing is removed and the connection closes. The rejection increments the private static `BODYLESS_FRAMING_REJECTED`. |
| `eqproxy-net/src/http2.rs` | `push_connection_tokens` and `request_connection_tokens` collect every `Connection` token (comma split, trim, lowercase, deduplicated). `response_headers` pre-scans the whole head and drops the fixed hop-by-hop list and every nominated name; `build_request_head` filters an h2 `HeaderMap` with repeated `connection` values. `http3.rs` uses both helpers. |
| `eqproxy-net/src/proxy.rs` | `Pool::checkout` polls the idle backend socket with `http1::peer_gone` before counting it as reused; a closed peer is dropped and a fresh connection is dialed. This closes the h2c → h1 preflight `502` seen by S12. |
| `eqproxy-net/tests/e2e_http2.rs` | The access-log fixture used `204` + `Content-Length: 0`, now deliberately `502`; it was changed to a legal bodyless `204`. The test's access-log purpose is unchanged. |
| `scripts/evidence/adversarial-2026-11-09/run_lab.py` (local-only; moved out of `docs/`) | S12 h2 uses raw h2c frames (legal HPACK/frame syntax) because a conforming h2 client removes `Connection` before sending. S12 h1 uses the real HTTP/1.1 listener with a preflight, a positive control, `Connection: x-evil` + `x-evil`, and the framing refusal case. |

## Unit and contract tests

- `bodyless_responses_reject_body_framing_and_close_on_metadata` → **1 passed**.
  Covers `204`+TE, `204`+CL, `101`+CL, `304`+TE, `HEAD`+TE; `304`/`HEAD`+CL
  remain legal and force a backend close; a normal `200`+CL and a plain `204`
  stay poolable; the HTTP/1.0 TE carve-out is explicit.
- `response_headers_drop_connection_nominated_names` → **1 passed**.
  Covers nomination before and after the named header, multiple tokens, and
  preservation of a normal header.
- `build_request_head_drops_connection_nominated_names` → **1 passed**.
  Covers repeated `Connection` values and a normal header.
- `access_log_marks_h2_requests_and_their_status` → **1 passed** after the
  legal `204` fixture.
- M-01 regressions: `chunked_transfer_encoding_is_rejected_in_http10` → **1
  passed**; `http10_` in `e2e_http1` → **2 passed**.

## Real E2E lab

`python3.13 scripts/evidence/adversarial-2026-11-09/run_lab.py` exited
**0** with **14/14** scenarios passed. The raw evidence is
[`v095-p0-3-p0-4-results.json`](v095-p0-3-p0-4-results.json).

Relevant S12 observations from the run:

- `S12_h2_request_connection_nominated_header`: both raw h2c cases were
  `PROTOCOL_REJECTED`; the origin saw no request and no forbidden header.
- `S12_h1_request_connection_nominated_header`: `preflight=True`,
  `control=True`. The origin received `host` and `x-keep` only for the
  nominated-header case; `Connection: x-evil, content-length` returned `400`
  and did not reach the origin.
- `S9_backend_204_with_transfer_encoding_does_not_desync`: first status `502`,
  second status `200`, body `NORMAL2`, forged body not served.
